How freelance rates work

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate?

Start with the take-home pay you want, gross it up for taxes, then add business costs, health insurance and retirement savings. Divide that total by the hours you can realistically bill in a year, not the hours you work. RateFloor does this for you.

Why can't I just divide a salary by 2,080 hours?

A 40-hour week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours, but freelancers don't bill all of them. Time off, sales, admin, invoicing and gaps between projects are unpaid, and you also cover your own taxes and benefits. Using only billable hours prevents underpricing.

What share of my time is billable?

It varies by field and by how much marketing and admin you do. Many freelancers start with an assumption around half to two-thirds and adjust after tracking their own time for a few weeks. Change the billable share in the calculator to see how sensitive your rate is.

What tax rate should I enter?

Enter your own combined estimate of income tax and self-employment tax. This depends on your country, region and income, so check with an accountant or your tax authority's guidance. The default is only an example.

Is this the rate I should charge?

It is a floor, the lowest rate that meets your income goal under your assumptions. Market demand, your experience and the value you deliver may justify charging more.
