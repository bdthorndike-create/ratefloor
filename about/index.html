About RateFloor

A free calculator for working out the lowest freelance rate that still gets you the income you want.

What RateFloor does

RateFloor is a free calculator for freelancers. You enter the take-home pay you want, your estimated tax rate, your yearly business costs and how much of your working time you can bill. It returns the lowest hourly, day and project rate that covers all of it.

How the number is worked out

Take-home pay is grossed up for taxes, then yearly health insurance, business costs and retirement savings are added. A profit buffer covers slow periods. The total is divided by the hours you can realistically bill, which is working weeks times weekly hours times your billable share. Dividing by billable hours, not all working hours, is what stops freelancers underpricing.

The formula is: (take-home ÷ (1 − tax rate) + costs + insurance + retirement) × (1 + buffer) ÷ billable hours.

Where the guides get their numbers

The rate guides use published median pay from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics as context. Those figures describe employees, not freelancers, so the guides explain what a freelance rate needs to add. Every default in the calculator is an example. Replace each one with your own numbers.

Your numbers stay with you

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to us. The share button puts your inputs in the web address after the # symbol, which browsers do not send to servers.

How the site pays for itself

RateFloor is free to use. It earns money from advertising and from Amazon affiliate links on the travel gear guide. Those links go to searches on Amazon and never change the calculator’s result. We have not tested the products named, and the guide says so.

Limits

RateFloor gives general estimates, not tax, legal or financial advice. Tax rules and rates vary by place and situation, so ask an accountant before you set prices. Questions or corrections are welcome on the contact page.
