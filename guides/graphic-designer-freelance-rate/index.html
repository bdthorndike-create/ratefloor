Freelance Graphic Designer Rate Guide

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports a median annual pay of $62,960 for graphic designers (May 2025 data). BLS reports that about 20 percent of graphic designers are self-employed.

Why that number is not your freelance rate

Spread across a standard 2,080-hour paid year, $62,960 works out to about $30.27 per hour (my arithmetic, not a BLS figure). An employee's employer also typically pays for benefits, equipment and unbillable time. As a freelancer you pay for all of that yourself, plus self-employment taxes, and you only earn for the hours you actually bill. That is why a freelance rate that matches an employee's pay has to be noticeably higher than the hourly equivalent.

What to add to your rate

Designers often pay for software subscriptions, stock assets, fonts and storage, and spend unbilled time on revisions and portfolio work. Setting a clear revision limit in each contract protects your effective hourly rate.

Try your own numbers

Open the calculator with $62,960 as a take-home goal. The other inputs are example values, so replace them with your own tax rate, insurance, costs, time off and billable share.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, graphic designers. Median pay is for employees across the United States and varies by location, experience and employer. This is general information, not tax or financial advice.
