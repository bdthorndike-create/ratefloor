Travel Work Gear for Freelancers

Connectivity, power and a place to work: the small kit freelancers pack to keep working on the road, and over the holidays.

Disclosure: some links below are Amazon affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. I have not tested these products. The list reflects items that several published freelancer and digital nomad gear lists mention again and again.

Portable Wi-Fi hotspot or travel router

Hotel and cafe Wi-Fi fails at the worst moment, usually right before a client call. A pocket hotspot or travel router gives you a backup connection you control. Check that it works with carriers where you are going. Browse portable hotspots on Amazon

Universal travel adapter

Outlet types change from country to country. A single adapter with USB-C ports covers most trips and keeps you from buying one at the airport. Browse travel adapters on Amazon

Power bank

A charged laptop and phone are your whole office. A power bank that can charge a laptop keeps you working through delays, long train rides and outlet-free cafes. Browse laptop power banks on Amazon

Noise-canceling headphones

Airports, shared spaces and busy cafes are loud. Noise canceling makes calls and focused work possible almost anywhere, and it is a popular gift. Browse noise-canceling headphones on Amazon

Portable SSD

Client files should not live on one laptop. A small external drive lets you back up projects and move large files quickly without relying on hotel internet. Browse portable SSDs on Amazon

Foldable laptop stand with a compact keyboard and mouse

Working hunched over a laptop for weeks is hard on your neck and wrists. A folding stand plus a small keyboard and mouse turns a borrowed table into a workable desk. Browse portable laptop stands on Amazon

Compact multi-port charger

One wall charger with several ports replaces a bag of power bricks, and you only need one outlet for your laptop, phone and headphones. Browse multi-port chargers on Amazon

Tech organizer pouch

Cables, adapters and drives scatter across a bag in minutes. A small organizer keeps them in one place and makes security checks easier. Browse tech organizers on Amazon

Carry-on backpack with a laptop compartment

Keep your laptop and everything you cannot replace with you, not in checked luggage. A bag that works as both a carry-on and a day bag makes travel days simpler. Browse laptop travel backpacks on Amazon

Count it in your rate

Gear you buy for work is a business cost, and your rate has to cover it. Put your yearly total in the "Software, equipment, other costs" field of the RateFloor calculator to see how it changes the hourly rate you need to charge.
