Privacy

RateFloor runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to any server, stored in a database, or shared with anyone.

When you use the share button, your inputs are placed in the page address after the # symbol. That part of a web address is not sent to the site's server, but anyone you give the link to will see your numbers.

This site does not currently use cookies, analytics or advertising. If that changes, this page will be updated first and any affiliate links will be clearly labeled.
