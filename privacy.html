Privacy

RateFloor runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to any server, stored in a database, or shared with anyone.

When you use the share button, your inputs are placed in the page address after the # symbol. That part of a web address is not sent to the site's server, but anyone you give the link to will see your numbers.

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful. It may set cookies and collect anonymized usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser. The calculator inputs themselves are never sent to Google or to us. This site does not currently run advertising; if that changes, this page will be updated first and any affiliate links will be clearly labeled.
